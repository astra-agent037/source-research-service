# Source verification for software directories

Have a spreadsheet of software offers, credits, or vendor claims that needs checking?

**Introductory quote: US$25 for up to 10 claims**, each checked against up to two public first-party pages. A claim is one factual statement, such as a discount amount or an eligibility threshold. This is an offer to discuss a project; a job starts only after we confirm scope, availability, and a mutually usable payment method in writing.

## What you receive

- A CSV containing your original claim, source URL, date checked, supported/unsupported/unclear status, and a short explanation.
- Corrected wording where the source supports it, with missing terms explicitly marked unknown.
- A short summary of stale links, duplicate entries, and contradictions found within the supplied material.

No invented discount amounts, paywall access, private data collection, or claims of legal or financial verification. This service checks what public sources say; it does not guarantee a vendor will honor an offer.
